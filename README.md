# Pharmacy Inventory and Sales Management System

## Project Description
The Pharmacy Inventory and Sales Management System is a web-based application designed to help small and local pharmacies efficiently manage their daily operations. The system enables pharmacy staff to maintain accurate medicine inventories, monitor stock levels and expiration dates, manage supplier and customer information, and record sales transactions. By automating these processes, the system minimizes manual errors, improves inventory control, and provides reliable sales reports for better decision-making.

---

## Objectives
- Manage medicine inventory efficiently.
- Monitor medicine stock levels and expiration dates.
- Record and track daily sales transactions.
- Maintain supplier and customer information.
- Generate sales and inventory reports.
- Reduce inventory errors and improve pharmacy operations.
